Preserving Flavor & Phytonutrients

Preparation & Storage Guide

Oyster mushrooms feature delicate gills that release their best flavor and retain key nutrients when kept dry and flash-cooked over medium-high heat.

1

Breathable Paper Bag

Store unwashed in a breathable brown paper bag in the main refrigerator compartment (34–38°F). Never seal in airtight plastic bags.

2

The Flash Sauté

Tear into bite-sized clusters. Dry-sear in a hot skillet for 2 minutes, then add olive oil or butter, minced garlic, and herbs until edges are crisp.

3

Heat Unlocks Nutrients

Gentle cooking breaks down tough chitin cell walls, liberating intracellular beta-glucans and proteins for superior digestive bioavailability.
