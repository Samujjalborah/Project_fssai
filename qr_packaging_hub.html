Printing Best Practices for Mushroom Packaging

  • ✓ Minimum Print Size: Print at least 2.0 cm × 2.0 cm (approx 0.8" × 0.8") to ensure quick scanning on low-light grocery store shelves.
  • ✓ Keep the Quiet Zone: Do not crop out the white margin around the QR code modules; smartphones require this contrast buffer.
  • ✓ Finish & Texture: Matte labels are preferable over high-gloss film to prevent camera glare when scanning under supermarket spotlights.
  • ✓ Call-To-Action (CTA): Always pair the QR code with a clear CTA like "Scan for Nutritional Value & Cooking Tips".
